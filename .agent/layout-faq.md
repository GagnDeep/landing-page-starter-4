# Layout Documentation: FAQ Page

## Section Architecture & Rhythm
A purely utilitarian page designed for quick answers.

### 1. Hero Section: How Can We Help?
- **Rhythm:** Minimalist, perhaps including a search bar.
- **Intent:** Direct users to answers immediately.

### 2. Category Jump Links
- **Rhythm:** Sticky sidebar or top bar.
- **Intent:** Navigation between General, Procedures, Billing, Emergency.

### 3. FAQ Accordion: General Questions
- **Rhythm:** Standard accordion component.
- **Intent:** Address opening hours, new patient processes.

### 4. FAQ Accordion: Procedures & Pain Management
- **Rhythm:** Standard accordion.
- **Intent:** Address anxiety about specific treatments.

### 5. FAQ Accordion: Insurance & Billing
- **Rhythm:** Standard accordion.
- **Intent:** Address administrative concerns.

### 6. Still Have Questions? (Contact CTA)
- **Rhythm:** Prominent box.
- **Intent:** If the answer isn't here, make it easy to call or email.

# Layout Documentation: Home Page

**Intent:** Establish the brand's premium positioning immediately. The home page balances the high-end emotional appeal of bridal makeup with the structural authority of a beauty academy.

## Sections
1. **Hero Header:**
   - *Rhythm:* Full viewport height (`min-h-screen`), expansive negative space.
   - *Components:* Large display typography (Cormorant Garamond), subtle background video/parallax image, centered concise tagline.
2. **Brand Anthem (About Snippet):**
   - *Rhythm:* Narrow content width (`max-w-3xl`), deep vertical padding (`py-32`).
   - *Components:* Large serif pull quote, small signature/founder note.
3. **Services Overview (Salon):**
   - *Rhythm:* Asymmetric grid (`grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2`), overlapping elements.
   - *Components:* High-quality imagery with soft borders, overlapping text cards.
4. **Academy Intro:**
   - *Rhythm:* Full bleed dark mode section to shift context visually.
   - *Components:* Bold typography, structured list of courses.
5. **Ajay Sir Profile (Expertise):**
   - *Rhythm:* Side-by-side layout, 50/50 split.
   - *Components:* Editorial portrait, structured bio, credential badges.
6. **Testimonials / Reputation (5.0 Stars):**
   - *Rhythm:* Carousel or horizontal scroll, ample horizontal padding.
   - *Components:* Star rating component, elegant serif quotes.
7. **Featured Work (Gallery):**
   - *Rhythm:* Masonry grid or curated 3x2 grid. Zero gap or `gap-1` for a modern editorial feel.
   - *Components:* Image hover states (slight scale).
8. **Call to Action (Booking/Enrollment):**
   - *Rhythm:* Centered, constrained width, heavy padding.
   - *Components:* Two primary buttons (Book Appointment, Explore Academy).
9. **Footer (Global):**
   - *Rhythm:* Multi-column grid, structured and hierarchical.
   - *Components:* Links, contact info, social icons, newsletter signup.
10-20. *Reserved for granular content blocks (e.g., specific service highlights, trust indicators, location map, instagram feed snippet, FAQ snippet, partner brands, etc. to be defined in content phase, all maintaining the 8rem vertical rhythm).*

# Layout: Home Page

## Structural Intent
The home page serves as the primary entry point, designed to establish immediate trust, showcase expertise (pediatric & painless treatments), and guide the user seamlessly towards booking a consultation.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Top Announcement Bar:**
   - *Intent:* Urgent or important notices (e.g., "Accepting new patients," "COVID safety protocols," "Holiday hours").
   - *Rhythm:* Full width, compact height, high contrast background (e.g., secondary or muted color).

2. **Global Navigation (Header):**
   - *Intent:* Clear, accessible routing.
   - *Rhythm:* Logo left, nav links center, CTA ("Book Appointment") right. Sticky on scroll.

3. **Hero Section:**
   - *Intent:* Emotional connection. Establish the "painless, family-trusted" value proposition.
   - *Rhythm:* Split layout or large background imagery with an overlapping content card. H1, subheadline, Primary CTA, Secondary CTA.

4. **Trust/Social Proof Bar (Logos/Badges):**
   - *Intent:* Immediate credibility.
   - *Rhythm:* Horizontal scrolling or grid of accreditations (e.g., ADA, local awards) and high-level stats (e.g., "10+ Years," "5000+ Smiles").

5. **Introduction / Problem-Solution:**
   - *Intent:* Address common dental anxieties. Emphasize "painless" and "comfort".
   - *Rhythm:* Centered, narrative-driven text block with ample whitespace.

6. **Core Services Overview (Grid):**
   - *Intent:* Highlight key offerings quickly.
   - *Rhythm:* 3 or 4 column grid of cards. Each card has an icon/illustration, title, brief description, and a "Learn More" link. (e.g., General, Pediatric, Cosmetic, Orthodontics).

7. **Spotlight: Pediatric Dentistry:**
   - *Intent:* Focus on a primary specialty. Reassure parents.
   - *Rhythm:* Asymmetric layout. Image of happy child/dentist on one side, soft-toned background, focused copy on the other.

8. **Spotlight: Painless Root Canals:**
   - *Intent:* Tackle the most feared procedure with reassuring facts and methodology.
   - *Rhythm:* Alternating from the previous spotlight. Image/graphic demonstrating advanced tech or calming environment.

9. **The Clinic Experience (Video/Tour):**
   - *Intent:* Show, don't just tell. A glimpse into the clean, modern facility.
   - *Rhythm:* Large central video thumbnail or image gallery slider.

10. **Why Choose Us (Features):**
    - *Intent:* Differentiators.
    - *Rhythm:* Icon list or a staggered grid. Features like "State-of-the-art tech", "Flexible financing", "Weekend appointments".

11. **Meet the Founder (Dr. Sarabjeet Singh):**
    - *Intent:* Personal connection and authority.
    - *Rhythm:* Portrait image paired with a bio, qualifications, and a personal quote.

12. **Patient Testimonials (Carousel):**
    - *Intent:* Peer validation.
    - *Rhythm:* Carousel of review cards. Star ratings, snippet of review, patient name.

13. **Before & After Gallery Preview:**
    - *Intent:* Visual proof of cosmetic and orthodontic results.
    - *Rhythm:* Interactive slider (before/after) or a clean grid of 4-6 transformations.

14. **How It Works (Patient Journey):**
    - *Intent:* Demystify the process from booking to aftercare.
    - *Rhythm:* 3-4 step process horizontal layout or vertical timeline. (1. Book, 2. Consult, 3. Treatment, 4. Smile).

15. **Insurance & Financing Options:**
    - *Intent:* Address financial concerns early.
    - *Rhythm:* Clean list of accepted insurances or a block explaining payment plans.

16. **FAQ Snippets:**
    - *Intent:* Answer top 3-4 questions immediately to reduce friction.
    - *Rhythm:* Accordion style.

17. **Latest from the Blog (Educational Content):**
    - *Intent:* SEO and demonstrating ongoing expertise.
    - *Rhythm:* 3-column grid of recent article cards (Thumbnail, Title, Excerpt).

18. **Final Call to Action (Pre-Footer):**
    - *Intent:* The ultimate conversion point.
    - *Rhythm:* Large, impactful banner. Strong headline ("Ready for your perfect smile?"), primary booking button.

19. **Location & Contact Quick-View:**
    - *Intent:* Practical logistics.
    - *Rhythm:* Map embed alongside address, phone number, and operating hours.

20. **Global Footer:**
    - *Intent:* Secondary navigation, legal links, social media.
    - *Rhythm:* Multi-column layout. Footer brand logo, sitemap columns, newsletter signup (optional), copyright/legal at the very bottom.

# Home Page Layout Structure

## Goal
A highly engaging, high-conversion landing page structured logically with clear rhythm, physical presence, and spacing following the "Glam-Academy" ethos.

## Sections (20 Distinct Sections)

1. **Hero (Immersive Entrance)**
   - **Intent:** High visual impact. Welcome to the Glam Academy.
   - **Visual Rhythm:** Full viewport height, massive typography (`h1`), high-contrast dark overlay over a stunning background.

2. **Trust Bar (Social Proof)**
   - **Intent:** Establish credibility immediately.
   - **Visual Rhythm:** Subtle, minimal height, monochromatic logos, slow pan animation.

3. **Core Philosophy / Manifesto (Statement)**
   - **Intent:** Introduce the brand's ethos.
   - **Visual Rhythm:** Expansive whitespace, large pull quote, center-aligned, rich typography.

4. **Split Feature: The Salon (Services)**
   - **Intent:** Highlight the bridal / salon services.
   - **Visual Rhythm:** Asymmetrical 60/40 split, image-heavy, subtle parallax, button with a hover scale.

5. **Split Feature: The Academy (Education)**
   - **Intent:** Highlight the education and recruitment side.
   - **Visual Rhythm:** Reversed 40/60 split for contrast, structured list of outcomes.

6. **Founder Spotlight**
   - **Intent:** Humanize the brand, build trust.
   - **Visual Rhythm:** Editorial magazine style layout, overlapping image and text blocks, signature font for the name.

7. **Curriculum Overview (Academy Focus)**
   - **Intent:** Quick glance at what students learn.
   - **Visual Rhythm:** Grid of 3-4 structured cards, distinct border-radius, clean icons.

8. **Featured Artists / Top Alumni**
   - **Intent:** Show success stories.
   - **Visual Rhythm:** Carousel or masonry layout, rich imagery, hover overlays showing names.

9. **The Bridal Experience (Salon Focus)**
   - **Intent:** Emotional connection to the bridal journey.
   - **Visual Rhythm:** Full-width image break, soft gradients, elegant text box centered.

10. **Testimonials (The "Glam" Reviews)**
    - **Intent:** Reassure potential clients/students.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** Large cards, overlapping the section borders, quote marks as background graphics.

11. **Statistics / "By the Numbers"**
    - **Intent:** Quantify success (e.g., "500+ Brides", "50+ Masterclasses").
    - **Visual Rhythm:** Dark mode contrast block, massive numbers with subtle counting animation.

12. **Gallery / Portfolio Teaser**
    - **Intent:** Visual proof of work.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** Tight grid, no gaps, high-quality images, subtle zoom on hover.

13. **Pricing Tiers / "Invest in Your Craft"**
    - **Intent:** Clear expectations for courses or primary services.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** 3-column layout, middle column highlighted/elevated.

14. **Process / "How It Works"**
    - **Intent:** Step-by-step journey for booking or enrolling.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** Vertical timeline or 4-step horizontal process with connecting lines.

15. **FAQ Teaser**
    - **Intent:** Address immediate objections.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** Accordion style, minimal borders, clear plus/minus icons.

16. **Location / The Studio**
    - **Intent:** Establish physical presence in Patiala.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** Map integration or studio imagery, address card overlapping the image.

17. **Newsletter / "The Insider List"**
    - **Intent:** Lead capture.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** Soft secondary background, simple clean input field, inline button.

18. **Latest from the Blog / Insights**
    - **Intent:** SEO and continued engagement.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** 3-card layout, date tags, read more links.

19. **Final CTA (The Push)**
    - **Intent:** Ultimate conversion point.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** Massive typography, vibrant primary background, large contrasting button.

20. **Footer**
    - **Intent:** Navigation, legal, and secondary links.
    - **Visual Rhythm:** Multi-column, dark background, subtle muted text, social icons.

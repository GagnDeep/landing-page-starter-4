# Layout: Home Page (`/`)

This document defines the structural blueprint and visual rhythm for the Home page. The layout balances high-end salon editorial imagery with clear conversion paths.

## 01. Pre-Header Announcement
- **Intent**: Small, dismissible banner for urgent updates or promos (e.g., "Now taking NRI bridal bookings for Winter 2024").
- **Visual Rhythm**: Very tight, high contrast.

## 02. Navigation Header (Transparent)
- **Intent**: Brand identity (left), main links (center), booking CTA (right). Merges with the Hero section initially.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Airy, refined, elegant typographic scale.

## 03. Hero Section (Immersive)
- **Intent**: Establish the "Boho Salon" aesthetic immediately. Full-height or 90vh.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Deep background (perhaps a dark teal or warm espresso tint over a lifestyle video/image), large, soft serif typography (`Playfair Display`), clear primary CTA.

## 04. Brand Ethos (Introductory Text)
- **Intent**: A short, poetic statement about the salon's philosophy.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Generous padding (top/bottom), large text size, centered, max-width constrained for readability.

## 05. Service Pillars (The Grid)
- **Intent**: Introduce the core specialties: Chrome Gel Nails, Curly Hair, Luxury Grooming.
- **Visual Rhythm**: 3-column asymmetric grid. Soft rounded cards (large radius), subtle tinted backgrounds.

## 06. Featured Artist/Stylist
- **Intent**: Highlight expertise and build trust through a human face.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Split layout (50/50 image and text). Image uses arched or soft-edged borders.

## 07. "The Boho Experience" (Video/Image Marquee)
- **Intent**: Show, don't tell. A horizontal scroll or marquee of ambient salon moments.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Edge-to-edge container, continuous slow movement, breaking the static grid.

## 08. Signature Service Spotlight (Chrome Nails)
- **Intent**: Deep dive into the most popular service.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large overlapping elements (image over text box), terracotta accent colors.

## 09. Signature Service Spotlight (Curly Hair)
- **Intent**: Deep dive into the second most popular service.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Reverse layout of Section 08. Dark mode section for high contrast and drama.

## 10. Client Testimonials (Carousel)
- **Intent**: Social proof from high-end/NRI clients.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Elegant slider, minimal controls, serif quotes, subtle sage green (`Accent`) accents.

## 11. Instagram Feed / Gallery
- **Intent**: Live portfolio of recent work.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Masonry or tight grid of images. No text, purely visual.

## 12. "Book Your Appointment" (Mid-page CTA)
- **Intent**: Re-engage user for conversion.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Full width banner, soft background color (Muted), prominent button.

## 13. Location & Atmosphere
- **Intent**: Highlight Sector 17, Chandigarh location.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Split layout. Map or exterior shot alongside address and opening hours.

## 14. Luxury Products We Use
- **Intent**: Establish quality by association (e.g., Olaplex, Dyson, high-end nail brands).
- **Visual Rhythm**: Logo garden, low opacity, centered in a wide, airy container.

## 15. FAQ Teaser
- **Intent**: Address immediate common objections or questions.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Accordion style, clean lines, plenty of white space.

## 16. Newsletter/Updates Opt-in
- **Intent**: Capture leads who aren't ready to book.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Simple form field + button.

## 17. Award/Press Recognition (If applicable)
- **Intent**: Additional authority.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Small badges or text mentions, very subtle.

## 18. Pre-Footer / Final CTA
- **Intent**: Last chance to convert before the footer.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large, bold typography, warm background.

## 19. Main Footer
- **Intent**: Navigation, legal, social links.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Multi-column, dark espresso background, light text for high contrast grounding.

## 20. Sub-Footer (Copyright)
- **Intent**: Legal requirements.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Very small text, lowest hierarchy.

# Home Page Layout Structure

The Home page must establish immediate trust, clinical excellence, and premium service. It should feature at least 20 distinct sections to provide a comprehensive overview of the clinic.

1.  **Global Navigation:** Sticky header with logo, primary links (Services, About, Pricing, Blog), and a CTA ("Book Consultation").
2.  **Hero Section:** High-impact, reassuring headline (using Playfair Display). Background visual suggesting a pristine, modern clinic. Primary CTA (Book Appointment) and secondary CTA (Learn More).
3.  **Trust Bar:** Row of logos/badges (awards, accreditations, associations).
4.  **Clinic Philosophy / Intro:** Brief manifesto on painless, high-precision care.
5.  **Core Services Grid (Overview):** 3-4 cards highlighting primary offerings (e.g., Implants, Cosmetic, General).
6.  **"The Experience" Section:** Step-by-step breakdown of what a patient can expect (Consultation -> Treatment -> Follow-up).
7.  **Doctor / Founder Bio Highlight:** Introduction to the lead specialist with a professional, reassuring portrait.
8.  **Sterilization & Technology Showcase:** Dedicated section explaining advanced, sterile protocols to build safety trust.
9.  **Before & After Gallery (Teaser):** Interactive slider or grid showing clinical success stories.
10. **Testimonial Carousel:** Highlighting 5-star reviews from premium patients.
11. **Virtual Tour Teaser:** Video or image gallery hinting at the luxurious, clean clinic environment in Sector 18A, Chandigarh.
12. **Amenities List:** Bullet points of patient comforts (e.g., calming music, sedation options, premium waiting lounge).
13. **Why Choose Us:** Icon-driven section (e.g., 20+ years experience, international standards).
14. **Membership / Financing Teaser:** Brief info on payment plans or premium memberships.
15. **Recent Articles / Blog Teaser:** 3 recent posts related to dental health and wellness.
16. **FAQ Accordion (Home Selection):** Top 3-5 most common questions for immediate reassurance.
17. **Location & Contact Map:** Interactive map of Sector 18A, address details, and clinic hours.
18. **Final Call to Action:** Large, prominent section inviting the user to book their first visit.
19. **Newsletter / Updates Signup:** Form to collect emails for dental tips.
20. **Global Footer:** Comprehensive links, legal links, social icons, and contact info.

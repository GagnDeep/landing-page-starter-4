# Layout: Home Page

## Structural Blueprint
The Home page serves as the grand entrance, balancing aesthetic allure with clinical credibility.

1. **Global Header (Navigation)**
   - *Intent:* Elegant, unobtrusive navigation. Sticky on scroll with a subtle glassmorphism effect.
   - *Rhythm:* `py-4 px-6 md:px-12`

2. **Hero Section (The Reveal)**
   - *Intent:* Breathtaking first impression. High-contrast typography over a serene, cinematic background (subtle video or high-end image of the clinic/smile).
   - *Rhythm:* Full screen height (`min-h-screen`), content vertically centered, heavy bottom padding.

3. **Trust Bar (Credentials)**
   - *Intent:* Immediate social proof and global standing. Logos of affiliations or subtle "As seen in" mentions.
   - *Rhythm:* `py-8`, muted opacity for logos, centered alignment.

4. **Introduction / Ethos Statement**
   - *Intent:* Dr. Sethi’s philosophy. A clean, minimal text area with a beautifully typeset oversized serif quote.
   - *Rhythm:* `py-24`, narrow max-width (`max-w-3xl`) for focused reading.

5. **Signature Treatments Grid (What we do)**
   - *Intent:* Showcase premium services (Veneers, Implants, Invisalign). Hover reveals.
   - *Rhythm:* 3-column asymmetric grid. `py-32`.

6. **The Clinic Experience (Visual Break)**
   - *Intent:* Full-bleed image or video snippet showing the luxurious interior of the Chandigarh clinic.
   - *Rhythm:* No padding (`p-0`), overlay text aligned to the bottom left.

7. **Smile Transformation Spotlight (Before/After)**
   - *Intent:* High-impact clinical proof, presented elegantly. Not a standard medical slider, but an editorial side-by-side or smooth mask reveal.
   - *Rhythm:* Split layout (text left, media right), `py-32`.

8. **UK to India Concierge Service (International Focus)**
   - *Intent:* Specifically address the UK clientele. Highlight travel, accommodation, and seamless care.
   - *Rhythm:* Card-based layout with a subtle background tint (`bg-muted`).

9. **Dr. Shobit Sethi Profile**
   - *Intent:* Establish authority. Portrait, credentials, and a signature.
   - *Rhythm:* 2-column layout (image left, text right), generous gap (`gap-16`).

10. **Testimonial Carousel (Stories of Change)**
    - *Intent:* Emotional connection. Beautifully typeset quotes without heavy card borders.
    - *Rhythm:* Centered, horizontal scroll or slow auto-fade.

11. **Clinical Precision (Technology & Hygiene)**
    - *Intent:* Reassure the medical aspect. Icons or abstract close-ups of advanced tech.
    - *Rhythm:* 4-column micro-grid, `py-24`.

12. **The Process (Step-by-Step)**
    - *Intent:* Demystify the journey. Numbered list with expanding details.
    - *Rhythm:* Vertical timeline layout.

13. **Featured Journal/Blog Posts**
    - *Intent:* Education and thought leadership.
    - *Rhythm:* 3-column standard grid, simple cards.

14. **Video Testimonial/Documentary Feature**
    - *Intent:* Deep dive into a patient journey.
    - *Rhythm:* Large embedded player, cinematic dark background section.

15. **FAQ Sneak Peek**
    - *Intent:* Address immediate concerns (pain, cost, travel).
    - *Rhythm:* Accordion layout, narrow width.

16. **Awards & Recognitions**
    - *Intent:* Reinforce elite status.
    - *Rhythm:* Badge grid.

17. **Newsletter / Guide Download**
    - *Intent:* Lead capture (e.g., "Download the UK Patient's Guide to Dental Tourism").
    - *Rhythm:* Inline form, contrasting background (`bg-primary`).

18. **Interactive Clinic Map / Location**
    - *Intent:* Show Chandigarh in a premium light. Custom styled map.
    - *Rhythm:* Full width.

19. **Primary CTA Module (Book Consultation)**
    - *Intent:* Final push. Bold, clear, and elegant.
    - *Rhythm:* Large typography, central button, `py-32`.

20. **Global Footer**
    - *Intent:* Comprehensive links, legal, contact info.
    - *Rhythm:* Multi-column, dark background, fine-print typography.

# Layout Documentation: Home Page

## Structural Intent
The homepage is the flagship experience of Amil's Hair Lounge. It operates as an editorial piece, guiding the user through the brand's expertise, the luxurious atmosphere, and the tangible results of their top-tier services.

## Sections (20+)

1. **Global Header (Navigation)**
   - Minimalist logo centered or left-aligned.
   - Transparent background on scroll, transitioning to solid.
   - Primary CTA: "Book Appointment".
2. **Hero Section (Cinematic Introduction)**
   - Full-screen or large high-impact imagery/video background (subtle motion).
   - Large Serif H1: "Elevate Your Everyday Elegance."
   - Subtitle: "Premium hair treatments and styling in the heart of Patiala."
   - Dual CTAs: "Book Now" (Primary) and "Explore Services" (Secondary Outline).
3. **Trust & Authority Bar**
   - Horizontal banner below hero.
   - Highlights: "4.9★ Google Rating", "Certified Keratin Specialists", "Luxury Products".
4. **Brand Ethos / Intro Statement**
   - Centered, editorial text block.
   - Serif font emphasis on "Affordable Luxury".
5. **Service Spotlight: Hair Treatments (The Core)**
   - Grid layout highlighting Hair Botox and Keratin.
   - Hover effects revealing before/after or brief descriptions.
6. **Service Spotlight: Color & Balayage**
   - Asymmetric image and text layout.
   - Focus on vibrant, healthy color results.
7. **Service Spotlight: Precision Cuts & Styling**
   - Clean card layout or carousel showing styling expertise.
8. **The "Why Us" Featurettes**
   - 3-column icon/text grid.
   - Focus on: Expert Stylists, Premium Products, Relaxing Atmosphere.
9. **Interactive Before & After Gallery**
   - Slider component showing dramatic transformations (Botox/Keratin focus).
10. **The Salon Experience (Atmosphere)**
    - Large cinematic image of the salon interior.
    - Parallax scrolling effect.
    - Text overlay describing the welcoming, trendy environment.
11. **Featured Specialists (Meet the Team Preview)**
    - Circular or soft-edge portraits of top stylists.
    - Brief credentials.
12. **Signature Treatment Deep-Dive**
    - Step-by-step visual of the Hair Botox process.
    - Educational focus to build trust.
13. **Client Testimonials (The Wall of Love)**
    - Masonry grid or smooth carousel of real reviews.
    - Highlighting 5-star ratings and specific treatment mentions.
14. **Product Partners / Brands We Use**
    - Logo carousel (e.g., L'Oréal, Olaplex, Kérastase).
    - Grayscale logos that gain color on hover.
15. **Pricing Teaser**
    - Clean, minimalist table or cards showing starting prices for key services.
    - Link to full pricing page.
16. **FAQ Snippet**
    - Accordion style for 3-4 common questions (e.g., "How long does Keratin last?").
17. **Promotional / Seasonal Offer Banner**
    - Full-width callout (e.g., "First-time client discount").
    - Contrasting background color (Secondary or Primary tone).
18. **Instagram Feed / Social Proof**
    - Grid of latest Instagram posts showing real-time salon work.
    - "Follow us @AmilsHairLounge".
19. **Newsletter / Insider Club Signup**
    - Elegant form field.
    - Offer: "Subscribe for hair care tips and exclusive booking slots."
20. **Location & Contact (Find Us)**
    - Embedded stylized map or high-quality exterior photo.
    - Address, opening hours, contact details clearly legible.
21. **Pre-Footer CTA (Final Push)**
    - Large typography: "Ready for your transformation?"
    - Prominent "Book Now" button.
22. **Global Footer**
    - Multi-column layout.
    - Quick links, service links, legal pages, social icons.
    - Copyright and subtle branding.

# Layout Documentation: Home Page

## Intent & Visual Rhythm
The home page is the digital entrance to the salon. It should feel immediately calming, high-end, and trustworthy. The rhythm moves from an immersive, emotional hero space into structured, easy-to-read proof points, followed by highly visual service explorations, and concluding with a strong, frictionless call to action.

## 1. Global Header (Navigation Shell)
- **Layout**: Fixed or sticky top navigation.
- **Rhythm**: Clean, minimal horizontal row. Logo on the left (or center), essential links in the middle, and a primary "Book Now" CTA on the right.
- **Visuals**: Glassmorphic or solid background adapting to scroll state.

## 2. Immersive Hero
- **Intent**: Establish the "Studio-Crafted" aesthetic immediately. Emotional connection over dense information.
- **Layout**: Asymmetric split or full-bleed image with an overlaid, elegant content block.
- **Visuals**: High-contrast Serif display typography. Subtle scaling motion on the background image. A clear, singular CTA (e.g., "Book Your Experience").

## 3. Trust Bar (Social Proof)
- **Intent**: Quickly validate the 4.9-star reputation without taking up too much vertical space.
- **Layout**: Horizontal banner.
- **Visuals**: Logos of publications, partner brands, or a cluster of 5-star Google review snippets. Uses the secondary surface color for subtle contrast.

## 4. The Studio & Doorstep Promise (Value Proposition)
- **Intent**: Clarify the dual-service model (in-salon vs. at-home) cleanly.
- **Layout**: Two large, side-by-side cards (or staggered blocks on mobile).
- **Visuals**: Elegant iconography or lifestyle imagery. Generous inner padding (`Base` or `Loose`).

## 5. Service Spotlight (Category Navigation)
- **Intent**: Let users visually browse what the salon offers (Hair, Skin, Nails, Bridal).
- **Layout**: Asymmetric grid or a smooth horizontal scroll (carousel) of elevated cards.
- **Visuals**: Sharp, high-quality imagery dominating the cards. Serif headings for the categories. Hover states that slightly dim the image and reveal a "View Services" link.

## 6. The "Your Home Salon" Difference (Features)
- **Intent**: Explain *why* to choose them (hygiene, vetted professionals, premium products).
- **Layout**: A 3-column grid (or 1-column on mobile) with generous spacing.
- **Visuals**: Minimalist icons, clean typography. Background uses `surface-secondary` to separate from the service grid.

## 7. Testimonials / The Experience
- **Intent**: Build deep trust through client stories.
- **Layout**: A curated, editorial-style slider or a masonry grid of quotes.
- **Visuals**: Focus on beautiful typography. Quotes use the Display font.

## 8. Final Booking CTA
- **Intent**: Frictionless conversion after they are convinced.
- **Layout**: Centered, spacious block with a high-contrast background (dark mode aesthetic even in light mode, or a rich accent color).
- **Visuals**: Large, inviting typography. A prominent, solid button.

## 9. Global Footer
- **Layout**: Multi-column grid.
- **Rhythm**: Brand column, Links column, Contact column, Social/Legal row at the bottom.

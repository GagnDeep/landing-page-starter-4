# Privacy Policy Layout Structure

Crucial for medical data trust.

1.  **Hero:** "Privacy Policy" and Last Updated date.
2.  **Table of Contents:** Sticky sidebar.
3.  **Data Collection:** What we collect (medical records, cookies).
4.  **Data Usage:** How patient data is used and protected.
5.  **Patient Rights:** Accessing or deleting records.
6.  **Contact for Privacy Queries.**

# Layout Documentation: Privacy Policy

## Section Architecture & Rhythm
Crucial for medical/dental websites to establish HIPAA/local healthcare data compliance.

### 1. Header
- **Rhythm:** Simple, text-only. Includes "Last Updated" date.
- **Intent:** Establish legality and compliance.

### 2. Patient Data Privacy Information
- **Rhythm:** Highlighted section or call-out box.
- **Intent:** Specifically address how medical records and contact info are kept secure.

### 3. Legal Content Body
- **Rhythm:** Long-form text, clear `h2` and `h3` dividers, standard spacing.
- **Intent:** Readability of general website tracking (cookies, etc.).

### 4. Contact for Privacy Concerns
- **Rhythm:** Small section at the bottom.
- **Intent:** Provide a point of contact for data deletion requests.

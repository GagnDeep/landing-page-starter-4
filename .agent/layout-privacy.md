# Privacy Policy Layout

## Intent
Standard legal compliance regarding patient data and website tracking.

## Sections
1. **Header:** Simple text header "Privacy Policy".
2. **Last Updated Date:** Text line.
3. **Content Blocks:** Standard legal text formatting (H2s and paragraphs) covering Data Collection, Use of Information, Patient Confidentiality (HIPAA equivalent for India), Cookies, and Contact Information for privacy concerns.

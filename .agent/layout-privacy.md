# Layout: Privacy Policy Page

## Structural Intent
To clearly outline how patient data is handled, maintaining the trust established elsewhere on the site. Crucial for healthcare compliance (HIPAA principles, even if not fully covered here, the tone must be serious).

## Sections

1. **Simple Header:**
   - *Intent:* Clear page title.
   - *Rhythm:* Minimalist. "Privacy Policy" and "Last Updated Date".

2. **Content Container:**
   - *Intent:* Readability and trust.
   - *Rhythm:* Single narrow column. Clear typography.

3. **Key Sections:**
   - Information We Collect (Personal, Medical info submitted via forms, Cookies)
   - How We Use Your Information (Appointments, Communication)
   - Data Security Measures (Reassure patients their data is safe)
   - Third-Party Sharing (e.g., insurance providers, software vendors)
   - Patient Rights (Accessing or deleting data)
   - Contact details for Privacy Officer

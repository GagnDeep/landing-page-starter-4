# Layout Documentation: Careers Page

**Intent:** Attract top talent to the salon and academy. Professional and inspiring.

## Sections
1. **Header:**
   - *Rhythm:* `py-20`, background image of the team.
2. **Culture / Why Join Us:**
   - *Rhythm:* 3-column grid, `py-24`.
3. **Open Positions:**
   - *Rhythm:* List layout, `max-w-4xl`.
   - *Components:* Job title, department, location, "Apply" link.
4. **Application Process:**
   - *Rhythm:* Step-by-step horizontal or vertical flow.
5. **CTA:**
   - *Rhythm:* "Don't see a fit? Send your resume."

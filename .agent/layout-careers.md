# Layout: Careers (`/careers`)

This document defines the structural blueprint and visual rhythm for the Careers page.

## 01. Pre-Header Announcement
- **Intent**: Sitewide banner.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Tight, high contrast.

## 02. Navigation Header (Solid)
- **Intent**: Standard navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Solid background.

## 03. Careers Hero
- **Intent**: Inspire talent to join.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Dynamic image of teamwork, bold headline.

## 04. Why Work With Us?
- **Intent**: Core pitch to prospective employees.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Centered, readable text block.

## 05. Culture & Environment
- **Intent**: Describe the daily vibe.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Image heavy, masonry layout.

## 06. Benefits & Perks
- **Intent**: List tangible advantages.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Grid of icons and short descriptions.

## 07. Education & Growth
- **Intent**: Emphasize continuous training.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Split layout (text/image).

## 08. Employee Testimonial 1
- **Intent**: Social proof from staff.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Quote block with employee photo.

## 09. Employee Testimonial 2
- **Intent**: More social proof.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Alternate side quote block.

## 10. Current Openings Header
- **Intent**: Transition to job listings.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large typography separator.

## 11. Job Listing 1 (e.g., Senior Stylist)
- **Intent**: Detail a specific role.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Expandable accordion or dedicated card.

## 12. Job Listing 2 (e.g., Nail Technician)
- **Intent**: Detail a specific role.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Expandable accordion.

## 13. Job Listing 3 (e.g., Front Desk)
- **Intent**: Detail a specific role.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Expandable accordion.

## 14. Application Process
- **Intent**: Explain next steps.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Step-by-step visual tracker.

## 15. Apprenticeship Program
- **Intent**: Detail entry-level opportunities.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Distinct section with a softer background.

## 16. Diversity & Inclusion
- **Intent**: State commitment to a welcoming workplace.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Simple text block.

## 17. General Application Form
- **Intent**: Catch-all for unsolicited resumes.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Clean, minimal form layout.

## 18. Contact HR
- **Intent**: Alternative contact method.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Simple text link/email.

## 19. Main Footer
- **Intent**: Navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Dark background.

## 20. Sub-Footer
- **Intent**: Legal.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Minimal.

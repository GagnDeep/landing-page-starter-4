# Layout Documentation: Careers

## Intent & Visual Rhythm
Selling the salon as a premium workplace to attract top talent.

## 1. Hero: Join the Team
- **Layout**: High-energy or warm team photo with an inspiring headline.

## 2. Why Work With Us (Benefits)
- **Layout**: Grid of benefits (e.g., training, environment, pay).
- **Visuals**: Iconography and clean, punchy copy.

## 3. Open Positions
- **Layout**: List of job titles, location, and a "Apply" link.
- **Visuals**: Clean, tabular list or simple cards.

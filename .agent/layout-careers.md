# Layout: Careers Page

## Structural Intent
To attract top dental talent (dentists, hygienists, administrators) by showcasing the clinic's culture, benefits, and modern facilities.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:**
   - *Intent:* Inspiring call to join the team.
   - *Rhythm:* Image of the team collaborating or a happy workplace environment. "Join the Perfect Smile Family".

2. **Why Work With Us? (Culture):**
   - *Intent:* Highlight the work environment.
   - *Rhythm:* 3-4 blocks detailing the modern tech, supportive environment, and focus on patient care over quotas.

3. **Benefits & Perks:**
   - *Intent:* Outline compensation and extras.
   - *Rhythm:* Icon list (e.g., Competitive Pay, Health Insurance, Continuing Education allowance, PTO).

4. **Employee Testimonials:**
   - *Intent:* Social proof from current staff.
   - *Rhythm:* Carousel or small grid of quotes from current hygienists or admins.

5. **Open Positions (Job Board):**
   - *Intent:* Direct applicants to specific roles.
   - *Rhythm:* Accordion or list of roles. Clicking reveals description, requirements, and an "Apply Now" button.

6. **General Application Call to Action:**
   - *Intent:* Capture resumes even if a specific role isn't open.
   - *Rhythm:* "Don't see a fit? Send us your resume anyway."

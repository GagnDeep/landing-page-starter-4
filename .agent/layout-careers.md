# Careers Page Layout

## Intent
Attract top dental and administrative talent by showcasing a positive, growth-oriented work environment.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Join the House of Smiles Family".
2. **Culture & Benefits:** Why work here? (Mentorship, state-of-the-art tech, work-life balance).
3. **Testimonials (Staff):** Quotes from current employees.
4. **Open Positions:** Accordion or list of available roles (e.g., Associate Dentist, Hygienist, Receptionist).
5. **Application Form:** Upload resume and brief cover letter.

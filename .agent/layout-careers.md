# Careers Page Layout Structure

## Goal
Recruit top-tier talent for the salon and instructors for the academy.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Join the Elite" - aspirational imagery of artists at work.
2. **Why 99 Institute?:** 3-column benefit breakdown (Growth, Environment, Compensation).
3. **Culture / Behind the Scenes:** Image carousel or video placeholder.
4. **Open Positions:** List view of current openings with "Apply" buttons.
5. **Application Form:** A clean form specifically for uploading resumes and portfolios.

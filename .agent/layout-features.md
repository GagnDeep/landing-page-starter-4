# Features / Services Page Layout Structure

## Goal
Detail the specific offerings of the Salon and the Academy, structured clearly for users looking for specific treatments or courses.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** Split title "Salon & Academy" with a toggle or dual-action buttons.
2. **Salon Services Menu:** List or grid of core services (Bridal, Party, Hair).
3. **Signature Treatments:** Highlight reel of the most premium offerings.
4. **Academy Curriculum:** Breakdown of courses (Beginner, Masterclass).
5. **Tools of the Trade:** Section highlighting the premium brands used (e.g., MAC, Huda).
6. **Service FAQ:** Quick accordion for common service questions.
7. **Book an Appointment / Enroll (CTA):** Large call to action section.

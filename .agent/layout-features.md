# Layout Documentation: Features (Services) Page

## Section Architecture & Rhythm
This page acts as a comprehensive catalog of services, structured for easy scanning but allowing for deep dives into specific treatments.

### 1. Hero Section: Comprehensive Care
- **Rhythm:** Clean, straightforward headline.
- **Intent:** Establish that the clinic handles everything from routine to complex care.

### 2. Service Category Navigation (Jump Links)
- **Rhythm:** Sticky horizontal bar or side menu on desktop.
- **Intent:** Quick navigation to General, Cosmetic, Orthodontic, etc.

### 3. General Dentistry Segment
- **Rhythm:** Grouped list or cards (Exams, Cleanings, Fillings).
- **Intent:** Bread-and-butter services.

### 4. Cosmetic Dentistry Segment
- **Rhythm:** High-visual emphasis (Before/After subtle examples).
- **Intent:** Aspirational services (Whitening, Veneers).

### 5. Specialized Focus: Orthodontics & Braces
- **Rhythm:** Dedicated block, distinct background color.
- **Intent:** Highlight a key clinic strength.

### 6. Specialized Focus: Painless Root Canals & Endodontics
- **Rhythm:** Reassuring typography, focus on technology used.
- **Intent:** Alleviate fear of complex procedures.

### 7. Pediatric Dentistry Segment
- **Rhythm:** Softer styling, focus on gentle care.
- **Intent:** Reassure parents.

### 8. Technology Highlight
- **Rhythm:** Icon and short text grid.
- **Intent:** Justify the quality of the services through the tools used.

### 9. Call to Action
- **Rhythm:** Standard block.
- **Intent:** Prompt booking for a specific consultation.

# Layout Documentation: Features & Standards

## Intent & Visual Rhythm
This page details the hygiene standards, product quality, and convenience aspects. It needs to be informative but remain visually luxurious, avoiding a dense "technical documentation" feel.

## 1. Hero: Uncompromising Quality
- **Intent**: Establish the high standards immediately.
- **Layout**: Left-aligned text with a supportive hero image on the right.

## 2. Hygiene & Safety Protocols
- **Intent**: Reassure clients about safety (crucial for home services).
- **Layout**: Icon-led list or a 2-column grid.
- **Visuals**: Clean, sharp iconography. High contrast for readability.

## 3. Premium Products
- **Intent**: Showcase the high-end brands used.
- **Layout**: Logo cloud or a set of elegant product spotlight cards.
- **Visuals**: Subtle surface cards to frame the product imagery/logos.

## 4. The Doorstep Experience
- **Intent**: Step-by-step breakdown of how home services work.
- **Layout**: Vertical timeline or horizontal stepper.
- **Visuals**: Connecting lines, numbered steps using the Display font.

## 5. Booking CTA
- **Layout**: Standard prominent CTA block.

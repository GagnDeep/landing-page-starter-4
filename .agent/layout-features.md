# Layout: Features/Services Directory Page

## Structural Intent
A comprehensive overview of all dental services offered, acting as a hub to route users to specific, detailed service pages.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:**
   - *Intent:* Broad overview of capabilities.
   - *Rhythm:* Clean header, "Comprehensive Care for Every Smile".

2. **Categorized Service Overview (Navigation Aid):**
   - *Intent:* Quick jump links.
   - *Rhythm:* Horizontal row of pill buttons or icons (General, Cosmetic, Orthodontics, Restorative).

3. **Category 1: General & Preventive Care:**
   - *Intent:* Detail foundational services.
   - *Rhythm:* Section title, brief intro, followed by a grid of specific services (Checkups, Cleanings, Fillings).

4. **Category 2: Pediatric Dentistry:**
   - *Intent:* Highlight this key specialty.
   - *Rhythm:* Distinct background color to separate it. Grid of services (Sealants, Fluoride, Early Braces).

5. **Category 3: Restorative & Painless Root Canals:**
   - *Intent:* Focus on advanced, pain-free treatments.
   - *Rhythm:* Detailed spotlight section for Root Canals, followed by a list/grid for Crowns, Bridges, Implants.

6. **Category 4: Cosmetic Dentistry:**
   - *Intent:* Aesthetic improvements.
   - *Rhythm:* Highly visual section. Grid for Whitening, Veneers, Smile Makeovers.

7. **Technology & Comfort Features:**
   - *Intent:* Reiterate *how* services are delivered (the "painless" aspect).
   - *Rhythm:* Icon list (Sedation options, Laser dentistry, Ergonomic chairs).

8. **Patient Testimonial (Service Focused):**
   - *Intent:* Reassurance for complex procedures.
   - *Rhythm:* A single, highly impactful quote about a major procedure (e.g., an implant or root canal).

9. **Call to Action:**
   - *Intent:* Prompt scheduling.
   - *Rhythm:* "Not sure what you need? Book a consultation."

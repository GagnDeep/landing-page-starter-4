# Layout: Features/Specialties (`/features`)

This document defines the structural blueprint and visual rhythm for the Features/Specialties page, focusing on what makes Boho Salon unique.

## 01. Pre-Header Announcement
- **Intent**: Sitewide banner.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Tight, high contrast.

## 02. Navigation Header (Solid)
- **Intent**: Standard navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Solid background.

## 03. Features Hero
- **Intent**: Highlight the salon's unique value propositions.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Editorial split screen (large bold text left, striking image right).

## 04. The Boho Philosophy
- **Intent**: Deep dive into the brand ethos.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Long-form text block, narrow width, large drop cap.

## 05. Feature 1: The NRI Experience
- **Intent**: Explain tailored services for traveling clients.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large icon or illustration, asymmetric text placement.

## 06. Feature 2: Clean Beauty Standards
- **Intent**: Highlight product quality and hygiene.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Card layout with soft green accents.

## 07. Feature 3: Master Technicians
- **Intent**: Focus on staff training and expertise.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Photo grid of technicians at work.

## 08. The Ambience Gallery
- **Intent**: Visual proof of the luxury environment.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Masonry image grid.

## 09. Technology & Tools
- **Intent**: Showcase high-end equipment used.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Minimalist icons and brief descriptions.

## 10. Featured Review
- **Intent**: Strong social proof validating the features.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Massive, oversized quote in serif font.

## 11. Sustainability Efforts
- **Intent**: Highlight eco-friendly practices.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Earthy tones, subtle leaf motifs or organic shapes.

## 12. Membership/Loyalty Teaser
- **Intent**: Hint at recurring benefits.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Elevated card with a metallic or distinct border.

## 13. Behind the Scenes (Video)
- **Intent**: Dynamic look at the salon.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Full-width video player container.

## 14. Partner Brands
- **Intent**: Establish authority via high-end product lines.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Clean logo ribbon.

## 15. The Hygiene Guarantee
- **Intent**: Assure clients of safety protocols.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Highlight box with checkmarks.

## 16. Accessibility Info
- **Intent**: Practical info for all clients.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Simple, clear text layout.

## 17. Compare the Difference
- **Intent**: Subtle comparison with standard salons.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Two-column "Us vs Them" format.

## 18. Booking CTA
- **Intent**: Drive conversions.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Warm terracotta background, centered.

## 19. Main Footer
- **Intent**: Site navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Dark background.

## 20. Sub-Footer
- **Intent**: Legal info.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Minimal.

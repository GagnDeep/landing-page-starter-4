# Layout Documentation: Features (Services & Academy details)

**Intent:** Provide detailed, structured information about the offerings. Needs to look like a high-end menu or syllabus.

## Sections
1. **Header:**
   - *Rhythm:* Clean, minimal `py-20`, centered.
2. **Bridal & Salon Services Menu:**
   - *Rhythm:* 2-column list, dotted leader lines (like a restaurant menu).
   - *Components:* Service name, price, description.
3. **Academy Syllabus Overview:**
   - *Rhythm:* Accordion/Collapsible list, `max-w-3xl`.
   - *Components:* Course titles, duration, modules.
4. **Feature Highlights:**
   - *Rhythm:* Alternating image/text blocks.
   - *Components:* "Why choose us" points.
5. **Interactive Comparison (Salon vs Academy):**
   - *Rhythm:* Side-by-side sticky scroll or tabs.
   - *Components:* Tabbed navigation.
6. **CTA:**
   - *Rhythm:* Standard closing CTA block.

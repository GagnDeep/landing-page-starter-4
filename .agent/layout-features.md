# Features (Services) Page Layout

## Intent
A comprehensive directory of all clinical services offered, explaining the technology and painless approach.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Comprehensive Care for Every Smile".
2. **Service Categories Nav:** Sticky sub-nav to jump to specific treatments (General, Cosmetic, Ortho, etc.).
3. **General Dentistry:** Checkups, cleanings, fillings (Icon + description).
4. **Cosmetic Dentistry:** Whitening, veneers, smile makeovers.
5. **Emergency 24/7 Care:** Emphasize immediate availability.
6. **Advanced Technology Highlight:** 3D imaging, laser dentistry.
7. **Pediatric Care:** Special section on child-friendly treatments.
8. **Patient Education:** Why these services matter (short paragraphs).
9. **CTA:** "Schedule Your Consultation".

# Layout Documentation: Journal / Blog

## Intent & Visual Rhythm
The blog (or "Journal") should feel like an editorial beauty magazine. It is content-heavy but must remain airy and visually engaging.

## 1. Hero / Featured Article
- **Intent**: Highlight the most recent or important post.
- **Layout**: Large structural block. Image takes up significant space (e.g., 60% width), with the title, excerpt, and "Read Article" link beside or overlapping it.

## 2. Article Grid
- **Intent**: Browseable index of past articles.
- **Layout**: 2 or 3-column grid of article cards.
- **Visuals**: Clean cards. Image on top, subtle meta-data (date/category), strong Serif title, short excerpt. Hover effects subtly scale the image.

## 3. Newsletter / Subscribe
- **Intent**: Capture emails for marketing.
- **Layout**: Centered inline block between the grid and footer.
- **Visuals**: Simple input field paired with a sleek submit button.

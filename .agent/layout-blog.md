# Layout Documentation: Blog Page

Educational content hub for hair care tips, trends, and salon news to boost SEO and authority.
## Sections
1. **Header**
2. **Hero** - "The Lounge Edit: Hair Care Insights."
3. **Featured Article** - Large spotlight on the latest/most important post.
4. **Category Filters** - (Trends, Maintenance, Product Reviews).
5. **Article Grid** - Card layout with featured image, title, excerpt, and read time.
6. **Newsletter Signup** - "Never miss an update."
7. **Footer**

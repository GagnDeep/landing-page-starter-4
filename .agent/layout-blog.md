# Layout: Blog/Journal (`/blog`)

This document defines the structural blueprint and visual rhythm for the Blog/Journal page.

## 01. Pre-Header Announcement
- **Intent**: Sitewide banner.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Tight, high contrast.

## 02. Navigation Header (Solid)
- **Intent**: Standard navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Solid background.

## 03. Journal Hero
- **Intent**: Set an editorial tone.
- **Visual Rhythm**: "The Journal" in large serif, soft background.

## 04. Featured Article
- **Intent**: Highlight the latest or most important piece.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large hero-style card, spanning full grid width.

## 05. Category Filters
- **Intent**: Allow sorting by topic (e.g., Nails, Hair, Tips).
- **Visual Rhythm**: Horizontal scroll of pill-shaped buttons.

## 06. Article Grid Layout
- **Intent**: Container for posts.
- **Visual Rhythm**: 2 or 3 column CSS grid.

## 07. Post Card 1
- **Intent**: Standard post preview.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Image top, title, excerpt, "Read More" link.

## 08. Post Card 2
- **Intent**: Standard post preview.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Matches Post Card 1.

## 09. Post Card 3
- **Intent**: Standard post preview.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Matches Post Card 1.

## 10. Newsletter Signup Break
- **Intent**: Capture emails.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Full-width colored band breaking the grid.

## 11. Post Card 4
- **Intent**: Standard post preview.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Matches Post Card 1.

## 12. Post Card 5
- **Intent**: Standard post preview.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Matches Post Card 1.

## 13. Post Card 6
- **Intent**: Standard post preview.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Matches Post Card 1.

## 14. Pagination/Load More
- **Intent**: Navigate older posts.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Centered button or numbered links.

## 15. Popular Tags
- **Intent**: Alternative discovery method.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Cloud of small text links.

## 16. Instagram Teaser
- **Intent**: Cross-promotion.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Small grid of images.

## 17. Author Spotlight
- **Intent**: Highlight staff writers.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Small profile card layout.

## 18. Booking CTA
- **Intent**: Convert readers to clients.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Standard CTA banner.

## 19. Main Footer
- **Intent**: Navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Dark background.

## 20. Sub-Footer
- **Intent**: Legal.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Minimal.

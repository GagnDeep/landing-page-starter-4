# Blog Layout

## Intent
Provide valuable dental health tips, improve local SEO, and establish thought leadership.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "The House of Smiles Journal".
2. **Featured Post:** Large highlight of the latest or most important article.
3. **Categories Filter:** Tags like "Oral Hygiene", "Kids", "Cosmetic", "Emergency".
4. **Article Grid:** Masonry or standard 3-column grid of posts (Image, Title, Excerpt, Date).
5. **Newsletter Signup:** Inline banner to subscribe for updates.
6. **Pagination/Load More:** Standard blog navigation.

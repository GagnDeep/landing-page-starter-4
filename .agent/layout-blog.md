# Layout Documentation: Blog Page

## Section Architecture & Rhythm
The blog establishes authority and provides ongoing value to the community through oral health education.

### 1. Blog Index Hero
- **Rhythm:** Featured article prominently displayed (large image, excerpt).
- **Intent:** Highlight the most important or recent news.

### 2. Category Filter
- **Rhythm:** Horizontal pill navigation.
- **Intent:** Allow users to sort by 'Oral Hygiene', 'Kids Dental', 'Clinic News'.

### 3. Article Grid
- **Rhythm:** Standard 3-column masonry or grid layout. Cards with `radius-md`.
- **Intent:** Browseable catalog of articles.

### 4. Pagination / Load More
- **Rhythm:** Simple button, centered.
- **Intent:** Access older content.

### 5. Newsletter Sign-up
- **Rhythm:** Contained block, distinct background.
- **Intent:** Capture emails for ongoing marketing.

### 6. Individual Post Layout (Template)
- **Rhythm:** Narrow reading column (`max-w-prose`), large typography.
- **Intent:** Optimal reading experience. Includes author bio and 'Related Posts' at the bottom.

# Layout: Blog / Articles Page

## Structural Intent
To position the clinic as an authority on dental health, improve SEO, and provide valuable, educational content to patients.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:**
   - *Intent:* Introduce the resource center.
   - *Rhythm:* Clean title "Dental Health Insights" with a brief subtext.

2. **Featured Article:**
   - *Intent:* Highlight the most important or newest piece.
   - *Rhythm:* Large layout, spanning wider than standard cards. Big thumbnail, prominent title, author, and date.

3. **Category Filter/Navigation:**
   - *Intent:* Allow users to find relevant topics.
   - *Rhythm:* Horizontal scrolling or sticky sidebar with tags (e.g., Pediatric, Cosmetic, Tips, Clinic News).

4. **Recent Articles Grid:**
   - *Intent:* Display the bulk of the content.
   - *Rhythm:* Standard 3-column masonry or even grid. Cards with thumbnail, category tag, title, short excerpt, and "Read More" link.

5. **Newsletter Signup:**
   - *Intent:* Capture emails for ongoing engagement.
   - *Rhythm:* Subtle inline banner asking users to subscribe for monthly dental tips.

6. **Call to Action (Consultation):**
   - *Intent:* Convert readers into patients.
   - *Rhythm:* "Have questions about your oral health? Schedule a visit."

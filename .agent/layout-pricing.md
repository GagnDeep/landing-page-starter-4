# Layout Documentation: Pricing Page

**Intent:** Clear, transparent, but luxurious presentation of costs. Avoid cheap "SaaS" pricing tables; aim for a bespoke "investment" feel.

## Sections
1. **Header:**
   - *Rhythm:* `py-24`, centered. "Your Investment".
2. **Bridal Packages:**
   - *Rhythm:* Elegant cards, `gap-8`, `max-w-5xl`.
   - *Components:* Card with delicate borders, list of inclusions.
3. **Academy Tuition:**
   - *Rhythm:* Single wide card or structured table.
   - *Components:* Clear breakdown of fees, payment plans.
4. **A La Carte Services:**
   - *Rhythm:* Simple list format, minimal styling.
5. **FAQ (Pricing Specific):**
   - *Rhythm:* Accordion, `max-w-3xl`.
6. **Contact for Custom Quote:**
   - *Rhythm:* Highlighted block, subtle background color.

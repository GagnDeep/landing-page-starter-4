# Layout: Pricing & Insurance Page

## Structural Intent
To provide transparency regarding costs, payment plans, and accepted insurances, reducing anxiety around the financial aspect of dental care.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:**
   - *Intent:* Set a tone of transparency and accessibility.
   - *Rhythm:* "Transparent Pricing, Accessible Care."

2. **Insurance Information:**
   - *Intent:* List accepted networks clearly.
   - *Rhythm:* Grid of insurance provider logos or a clean, categorized list. Note on how the staff helps with claims.

3. **New Patient Specials:**
   - *Intent:* Low-barrier entry offers.
   - *Rhythm:* Highlighted cards (e.g., "$99 New Patient Exam & X-Rays").

4. **General Pricing Guidelines:**
   - *Intent:* Give ballpark figures for common procedures to build trust.
   - *Rhythm:* A clean, easy-to-read table or list (e.g., Checkups, Basic Cleanings, Teeth Whitening). *Note: Disclaimer that specific prices require consultation.*

5. **Financing Options (CareCredit, In-house plans):**
   - *Intent:* Show how to make care affordable.
   - *Rhythm:* Step-by-step explanation or detailed blocks for different financing partners.

6. **The Perfect Smile Membership Plan:**
   - *Intent:* Promote recurring revenue and uninsured patient care.
   - *Rhythm:* Tiered pricing cards (e.g., Child, Adult, Perio) showing monthly cost and included benefits.

7. **FAQ (Financial Focus):**
   - *Intent:* Address specific money questions.
   - *Rhythm:* Accordion (e.g., "Do I need to pay upfront?", "What if I don't have insurance?").

8. **Call to Action:**
   - *Intent:* Encourage booking despite financial concerns.
   - *Rhythm:* "Let's discuss your custom treatment plan."

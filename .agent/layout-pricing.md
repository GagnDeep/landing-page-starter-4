# Layout: Pricing (`/pricing`)

This document defines the structural blueprint and visual rhythm for the Pricing page.

## 01. Pre-Header Announcement
- **Intent**: Sitewide banner.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Tight, high contrast.

## 02. Navigation Header (Solid)
- **Intent**: Standard navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Solid background.

## 03. Pricing Hero
- **Intent**: Set expectations for luxury pricing.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Clean, minimal, large centered text: "Transparent Luxury."

## 04. Pricing Philosophy
- **Intent**: Explain why the prices are what they are (quality, time).
- **Visual Rhythm**: Short paragraph, italicized serif.

## 05. Quick Jump Links
- **Intent**: Navigate to specific pricing tiers.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Pill-shaped buttons in a horizontal row.

## 06. Tier 1: Essential Care
- **Intent**: Base level services.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Clean pricing table or list.

## 07. Tier 2: Signature Boho (Highlighted)
- **Intent**: The most popular, recommended tier.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Elevated card, distinct border, "Most Popular" badge.

## 08. Tier 3: Luxury/Bridal
- **Intent**: High-end, comprehensive packages.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Dark mode card for premium feel.

## 09. Add-on Services Menu
- **Intent**: List a la carte options.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Dense, clean list.

## 10. Memberships (If applicable)
- **Intent**: Recurring revenue options.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Side-by-side comparison cards.

## 11. Gift Cards Teaser
- **Intent**: Promote gifting.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Image of a physical gift card, brief text.

## 12. Cancellation Policy
- **Intent**: Clear communication of rules.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Subtle, muted text box.

## 13. Payment Methods Accepted
- **Intent**: Practical info.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Small row of payment icons.

## 14. What's Included
- **Intent**: Highlight complimentary extras (drinks, wifi).
- **Visual Rhythm**: Icon grid.

## 15. FAQ (Pricing Specific)
- **Intent**: Address cost-related questions.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Accordion.

## 16. Corporate/Group Rates Teaser
- **Intent**: Attract larger bookings.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Simple banner.

## 17. Financing/Installments (If applicable)
- **Intent**: Provide payment options for large packages.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Small subtext or badge.

## 18. Booking CTA
- **Intent**: Convert.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large button, generous padding.

## 19. Main Footer
- **Intent**: Site navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Dark background.

## 20. Sub-Footer
- **Intent**: Legal info.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Minimal.

# Pricing & Insurance Page Layout

## Intent
To provide transparent financial information, reducing anxiety and highlighting accessible care options.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** "Transparent Pricing, Accessible Care".
2. **Insurance Partners:** Grid of accepted insurance logos.
3. **General Pricing Guidelines:** Average costs for common procedures (consultations, cleanings). *Note: Keep it general, emphasizing that precise quotes require consultation.*
4. **Financing Options:** EMI plans, partner credit services.
5. **Membership/Loyalty Plan:** Details of any in-house discount plans for uninsured patients.
6. **FAQ (Financial):** "Do I pay upfront?", "How does insurance billing work?"
7. **CTA:** "Discuss Your Treatment Plan" (Contact link).

# Pricing Page Layout Structure

## Goal
Provide clear, transparent pricing for both Salon services and Academy courses, utilizing the "Glam-Academy" premium feel.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** Minimalist title, clean subtext "Investment in Excellence."
2. **Bridal Packages:** 3-tier pricing cards (Standard, Premium, Signature).
3. **Academy Tuition:** Detailed breakdown of course costs, duration, and what's included.
4. **A La Carte Services:** Clean, tabular layout for individual salon services.
5. **Payment Plans:** Information on financing for academy students.
6. **FAQ on Pricing:** Accordion specifically for payment terms, deposits, and refunds.
7. **Consultation CTA:** Push to book a free consultation before committing.

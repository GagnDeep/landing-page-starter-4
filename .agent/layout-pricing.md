# Layout Documentation: Pricing (Patient Information) Page

## Section Architecture & Rhythm
Dental pricing is often bespoke, so this page focuses on transparency, insurance, and the *value* of care rather than a rigid e-commerce price list.

### 1. Hero Section: Transparent Care
- **Rhythm:** Empathetic, clear messaging.
- **Intent:** Assure patients there are no hidden surprises.

### 2. Insurance Accepted
- **Rhythm:** Logo grid or organized list.
- **Intent:** Immediate practical utility for patients checking coverage.

### 3. Payment Plans & Financing
- **Rhythm:** Step-by-step or card layout.
- **Intent:** Explain options for out-of-pocket expenses (e.g., EMI options).

### 4. General Consultation Fees
- **Rhythm:** Simple, highly legible table or list.
- **Intent:** Provide baseline costs for initial visits and X-rays.

### 5. Why Quality Care Matters (Value Prop)
- **Rhythm:** Text block with supporting icon.
- **Intent:** Justify costs by emphasizing long-term health and material quality.

### 6. FAQ: Billing & Insurance
- **Rhythm:** Accordion.
- **Intent:** Answer specific administrative questions.

### 7. Call to Action
- **Rhythm:** Standard block.
- **Intent:** Book a consultation to get a precise treatment plan.

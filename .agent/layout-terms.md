# Layout Documentation: Terms of Service

## Section Architecture & Rhythm
A standard legal page. Must be highly legible.

### 1. Header
- **Rhythm:** Simple, text-only. Includes "Last Updated" date.
- **Intent:** Establish legality.

### 2. Legal Content Body
- **Rhythm:** Long-form text, clear `h2` and `h3` dividers, standard spacing.
- **Intent:** Readability.

### 3. Contact for Legal Inquiries
- **Rhythm:** Small section at the bottom.
- **Intent:** Provide a point of contact for disputes or questions.

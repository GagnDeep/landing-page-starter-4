# Layout: Terms of Service Page

## Structural Intent
To provide necessary legal documentation in a clean, readable format without detracting from the overall site aesthetic.

## Sections

1. **Simple Header:**
   - *Intent:* Clear page title.
   - *Rhythm:* Minimalist. "Terms of Service" and "Last Updated Date".

2. **Content Container:**
   - *Intent:* Readability.
   - *Rhythm:* Single narrow column for optimal reading measure. Standard body text with clear hierarchical headings (H2, H3).

3. **Key Sections (Standard Legal Boilerplate structured for readability):**
   - Acceptance of Terms
   - Use of the Website
   - Medical Disclaimer (Crucial for dental sites: "Information is not substitute for professional medical advice")
   - Intellectual Property
   - Limitation of Liability
   - Contact Information for Legal Inquiries

# Layout Documentation: About Page
## Structural Intent
Tells the story of Amil's Hair Lounge, focusing on the founders' vision, the salon's history, and the dedication to "affordable luxury."
## Sections
1. **Header**
2. **Hero** - "Our Story" with a warm, inviting image of the salon or founders.
3. **The Vision/Mission Statement** - Editorial typography.
4. **The Journey** - Timeline or narrative blocks detailing the salon's growth.
5. **Meet the Team** - Comprehensive grid of stylists with bios.
6. **The Space** - Image gallery focusing on the luxurious interior.
7. **Core Values** - 3-4 pillars (e.g., Quality, Community, Expertise).
8. **Join the Team / Careers Teaser** - Link to careers page.
9. **Final CTA** - "Experience it for yourself."
10. **Footer**

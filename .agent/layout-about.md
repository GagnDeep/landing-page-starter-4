# Layout: About Us Page

## Structural Intent
To tell the story of Perfect Smile Dental Clinic, focusing on its legacy, the team's expertise, and the core values that make it a trusted family choice.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:**
   - *Intent:* Establish the tone.
   - *Rhythm:* Large, warm image of the clinic or team, overlaid with a strong, welcoming headline (e.g., "Our Story").

2. **Our Mission & Vision:**
   - *Intent:* Clearly state the clinic's purpose.
   - *Rhythm:* Centered, large typography block emphasizing "painless care" and "community trust".

3. **The Clinic's History/Legacy:**
   - *Intent:* Show stability and long-term commitment to patients.
   - *Rhythm:* A vertical timeline or a narrative block with historical photos (e.g., "Over a decade of smiles").

4. **Meet the Founder (Deep Dive):**
   - *Intent:* In-depth background on Dr. Sarabjeet Singh.
   - *Rhythm:* Large portrait, detailed biography, specific qualifications, and personal philosophy.

5. **Meet the Team:**
   - *Intent:* Introduce associate dentists, hygienists, and support staff.
   - *Rhythm:* Grid layout of team member cards (photo, name, role, short bio).

6. **Our Core Values:**
   - *Intent:* What drives the practice.
   - *Rhythm:* 3-4 icon-based blocks (e.g., Empathy, Excellence, Integrity, Innovation).

7. **The Facility/Technology:**
   - *Intent:* Highlight modern, safe equipment.
   - *Rhythm:* Image gallery or a split layout detailing specific tech (e.g., digital X-rays, intraoral scanners).

8. **Community Involvement:**
   - *Intent:* Show the clinic gives back.
   - *Rhythm:* Brief narrative section with photos of community events or sponsorships.

9. **Call to Action (Join our family):**
   - *Intent:* Conversion.
   - *Rhythm:* Simple, centered CTA block encouraging visitors to book a consultation.

# About Page Layout Structure

Focus on the legacy, clinical standards, and the human element behind the practice.

1.  **Hero:** Title "Our Story" or "Clinical Excellence."
2.  **Mission & Vision:** Core values.
3.  **The Founders:** Detailed bios of lead doctors.
4.  **The Team:** Grid of supporting specialists and hygienists.
5.  **Our Facility:** Deep dive into the clinic's architecture and sterile environment.
6.  **Milestones / Timeline:** History of the clinic.
7.  **Accreditations:** Detailed view of certifications.
8.  **CTA:** Book an appointment.

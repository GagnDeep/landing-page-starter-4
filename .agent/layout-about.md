# Layout Documentation: About Page

## Intent & Visual Rhythm
The about page tells the story of the salon, its founders, and its philosophy. It should feel like a premium editorial piece in a magazine—rich with imagery, breathing room, and elegant typography.

## 1. Hero: The Philosophy
- **Intent**: Set the tone and state the core mission.
- **Layout**: Centered, highly spacious text block over a subtle background or alongside a large, artistic studio shot.
- **Visuals**: Very large Display typography for the main hook.

## 2. Our Story (Timeline or Narrative)
- **Intent**: How the salon started and its journey to being top-rated.
- **Layout**: Asymmetric text-and-image pairs alternating down the page.
- **Visuals**: Overlapping images, generous `Breathe` spacing between sections to encourage slow reading.

## 3. The Team / Our Experts
- **Intent**: Humanize the brand by showing the professionals.
- **Layout**: Clean grid of portrait cards.
- **Visuals**: Uniform, high-quality portraits. Hover effects reveal specialties or a short bio.

## 4. The Studio Space
- **Intent**: Showcase the physical location to entice in-salon visits.
- **Layout**: A curated masonry gallery or a large interactive carousel.
- **Visuals**: Edge-to-edge imagery with minimal text interference.

## 5. CTA: Experience It
- **Layout**: Simple, elegant block pushing users to book an appointment.

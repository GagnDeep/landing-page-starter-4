# Layout Documentation: About Page

**Intent:** Tell the story of Ajay sir and the evolution of the salon & academy. Focus on authority, luxury, and heritage.

## Sections
1. **Hero Header:**
   - *Rhythm:* `h-[60vh]`, text-heavy left alignment with subtle background graphic.
   - *Components:* Large H1, bold overline.
2. **The Founder's Story:**
   - *Rhythm:* `max-w-4xl` centered, `py-24`, generous line height.
   - *Components:* Editorial text flow, drop caps.
3. **Timeline / Milestones:**
   - *Rhythm:* Vertical rule, alternating left/right blocks.
   - *Components:* Dates in Serif, descriptions in Sans.
4. **Our Philosophy:**
   - *Rhythm:* 3-column grid (`gap-12`), standard padding.
   - *Components:* Icons/Minimal graphics, short paragraphs.
5. **Team Roster:**
   - *Rhythm:* 4-column grid, tight gaps.
   - *Components:* Headshots with name and title overlines.
6. **Location & Space:**
   - *Rhythm:* Full bleed image, overlapping text card.
   - *Components:* Interior photos of the Punjabi Bagh location.
7. **Call to Action:**
   - *Rhythm:* Standard closing CTA block.

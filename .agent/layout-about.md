# Layout Documentation: About Page

## Section Architecture & Rhythm
The About page tells the story of Dr. Bansal's Dental Care. It shifts the focus from purely clinical to personal, establishing the "friendly" in "friendly-professional".

### 1. Hero Section: The Clinic's Story
- **Rhythm:** Immersive image, strong heading.
- **Intent:** Set the tone. A welcoming image of the clinic exterior or the team.

### 2. Our Philosophy / Mission Statement
- **Rhythm:** Centered blockquote or large typography.
- **Intent:** Core values (e.g., patient comfort, community health).

### 3. Meet the Founder (Dr. Bansal)
- **Rhythm:** Split layout (Detailed Bio / Professional Headshot).
- **Intent:** Deep dive into qualifications, experience, and personal touch.

### 4. The Team (Hygienists, Assistants, Admin)
- **Rhythm:** Grid of smaller profile cards.
- **Intent:** Show that the whole staff is friendly and competent.

### 5. History / Timeline of the Clinic
- **Rhythm:** Vertical timeline.
- **Intent:** Show stability and community roots in Patiala.

### 6. Community Involvement
- **Rhythm:** Image-led, narrative text.
- **Intent:** Demonstrate commitment to the local neighborhood.

### 7. Call to Action
- **Rhythm:** Standard bottom CTA block.
- **Intent:** "Ready to meet us?" -> Book Appointment.

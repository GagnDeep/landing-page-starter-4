# Layout: About Us (`/about`)

This document defines the structural blueprint and visual rhythm for the About page.

## 01. Pre-Header Announcement
- **Intent**: Sitewide banner.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Tight, high contrast.

## 02. Navigation Header (Solid)
- **Intent**: Standard navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Solid background.

## 03. About Hero
- **Intent**: Introduce the salon's story.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large, warm hero image of the salon interior or founders.

## 04. The Origin Story
- **Intent**: Narrative text about the founding.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Editorial text layout, two columns.

## 05. Mission Statement
- **Intent**: Core purpose.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Oversized, centered serif text.

## 06. The Founders
- **Intent**: Humanize the brand.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Profile images with soft edges, bio text.

## 07. Meet the Team (Grid)
- **Intent**: Showcase key stylists/technicians.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Grid of portraits, names, and specialties.

## 08. Our Values
- **Intent**: Bullet points of brand values.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Icon + text list layout.

## 09. The Chandigarh Connection
- **Intent**: Local roots and pride.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Image of the city/sector 17 mixed with salon shots.

## 10. The Space (Studio Tour)
- **Intent**: Highlight the physical environment.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Carousel of interior shots.

## 11. Timeline/Milestones
- **Intent**: Show history and growth.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Vertical timeline design.

## 12. Press & Media
- **Intent**: External validation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Logos or article snippets.

## 13. Community Involvement
- **Intent**: Show social responsibility.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Text block with supporting image.

## 14. Partner Brands
- **Intent**: Highlight high-end associations.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Logo grid.

## 15. The Art of the Craft
- **Intent**: Focus on training and skill.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Action shots of staff working.

## 16. Join Our Team Teaser
- **Intent**: Recruitment hook.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Subtle banner linking to careers.

## 17. Client Testimonial
- **Intent**: Wrap up with social proof.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large quote block.

## 18. Booking CTA
- **Intent**: Convert.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Primary button, warm background.

## 19. Main Footer
- **Intent**: Navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Dark background.

## 20. Sub-Footer
- **Intent**: Legal.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Minimal text.

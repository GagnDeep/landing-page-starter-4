# About Page Layout

## Intent
To tell the story of Dr. Kochar's House of Smiles, establish trust, and emphasize the family-centered, 24/7 care philosophy.

## Sections
1. **Hero:** Title "Our Story", warm image of the clinic or founders.
2. **Mission & Vision:** Core values statement.
3. **Founder's Journey:** Dr. Kochar's background, education, and reason for starting the clinic.
4. **The Team:** Grid layout of dentists, hygienists, and support staff with brief bios.
5. **The Clinic Tour:** Photo gallery of the state-of-the-art, comforting facilities.
6. **Community Involvement:** Highlighting local Chandigarh initiatives.
7. **CTA:** "Join Our Patient Family" - Book appointment button.

# Layout: Services (`/services`)

This document defines the structural blueprint and visual rhythm for the Services page.

## 01. Pre-Header Announcement
- **Intent**: Sitewide banner for updates.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Tight, high contrast.

## 02. Navigation Header (Solid)
- **Intent**: Standard navigation.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Solid background, distinct from hero.

## 03. Services Hero
- **Intent**: Introduce the breadth of offerings.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Medium height, bold serif headline, subtle textured background.

## 04. Menu Navigation (Sticky)
- **Intent**: Quick jump links to service categories (Nails, Hair, Skin).
- **Visual Rhythm**: Horizontal scrollable bar, sticky on scroll.

## 05. Category 1: Nail Artistry (Header)
- **Intent**: Introduce the first category.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large typography, generous top margin.

## 06. Nail Services List
- **Intent**: Detailed list of nail services with pricing.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Minimalist list layout with dotted leaders connecting service to price.

## 07. Featured Nail Service (Highlight)
- **Intent**: Spotlight high-margin service (e.g., Chrome Gel Extensions).
- **Visual Rhythm**: Callout box with soft terracotta background and image.

## 08. Category 2: Hair Expertise (Header)
- **Intent**: Introduce hair services.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Matches Section 05.

## 09. Hair Services List
- **Intent**: Detailed list of hair services.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Matches Section 06.

## 10. The Curly Hair Specialty (Highlight)
- **Intent**: Highlight curly hair expertise.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Split layout (text/image), deep teal accent.

## 11. Category 3: Skin & Grooming (Header)
- **Intent**: Introduce skin/grooming.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Matches Section 05.

## 12. Skin Services List
- **Intent**: Detailed list of skin services.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Matches Section 06.

## 13. Bridal Packages (Header)
- **Intent**: Introduce packages.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Elegant, distinctly styled header.

## 14. Bridal Packages Details
- **Intent**: High-end package details.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Grid of cards, elevated styling.

## 15. The Consultation Process
- **Intent**: Explain how to book and what to expect.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Step-by-step visual (1, 2, 3) layout.

## 16. Service Add-ons
- **Intent**: Upsell small services.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Simple, tight grid of badges or small text.

## 17. Client Preparation Guide
- **Intent**: What to do before arriving.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Accordion or simple bulleted list in a tinted container.

## 18. Booking CTA
- **Intent**: Final push to book.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Large, centered, bold button.

## 19. Main Footer
- **Intent**: Site navigation and info.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Dark mode, multi-column.

## 20. Sub-Footer
- **Intent**: Legal info.
- **Visual Rhythm**: Minimal, small text.

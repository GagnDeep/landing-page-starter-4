# Layout: Contact Page

## Structural Intent
To make it as easy as possible for users to reach the clinic, find the location, and book appointments.

## Sections

1. **Hero Header:**
   - *Intent:* Direct and welcoming.
   - *Rhythm:* "Get in Touch" or "Schedule Your Visit".

2. **Primary Contact Information Cards:**
   - *Intent:* Immediate access to crucial info.
   - *Rhythm:* 3 Cards across the top: Phone (click-to-call), Email (mailto), Address (link to maps).

3. **Booking/Inquiry Form:**
   - *Intent:* Capture leads directly.
   - *Rhythm:* Clean, well-spaced form (Name, Email, Phone, Preferred Date/Time, Message). *Ensure clear validation.*

4. **Operating Hours:**
   - *Intent:* Clarity on availability.
   - *Rhythm:* A styled list or table showing days and hours, noting any weekend availability.

5. **Interactive Map & Directions:**
   - *Intent:* Help patients find the clinic easily.
   - *Rhythm:* Large embedded Google Map. Text directions for parking or landmarks (e.g., "Located near...").

6. **Emergency Dental Care Notice:**
   - *Intent:* Provide immediate instructions for urgent situations.
   - *Rhythm:* Highlighted or bordered box indicating what to do in a dental emergency outside normal hours.

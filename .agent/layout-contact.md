# Layout Documentation: Contact & Location

## Intent & Visual Rhythm
Practical but welcoming. Combines functional forms with location details.

## 1. Hero: Get in Touch
- **Layout**: Split layout. Left side: Contact form. Right side: Contact details and Map.

## 2. The Form
- **Intent**: Capture inquiries cleanly.
- **Visuals**: Minimalist input fields (perhaps just bottom borders or soft, rounded rectangles). High-contrast submit button.

## 3. Location & Hours
- **Intent**: Clear information for in-salon visitors.
- **Layout**: Structured text block.
- **Visuals**: Integration of a sleek, greyscale or custom-styled Google Map.
